About us

Family-run. Site-tested. Thirty years in.

  • Founded asA family business
  • Combined experience30+ years
  • Client baseDomestic & commercial
  • Notable clients5★ housebuilders

DesignScapes Southwest is a family-run team of skilled landscapers offering hard and soft landscaping, maintenance, fencing and tree surgery — with over 30 years of combined experience between us.

We've built our reputation project by project, staying involved from a site's inception right through to completion. That's earned us long-standing relationships with clients ranging from private homeowners to five-star national housebuilders on large commercial developments.

Every job is supervised by qualified contract and senior management, with ongoing training across the team — because the standard on-site should never depend on which day you visit.
